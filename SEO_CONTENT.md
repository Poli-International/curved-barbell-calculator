## What is the Curved Barbell Length Calculator?

The Curved Barbell Length Calculator is a free web tool that determines the correct internal length for curved barbell body jewelry. It calculates the recommended size by adding a healing-stage-specific clearance to your measured anatomy span (tissue depth).

The tool supports six piercing types: rook, daith, snug, conch, eyebrow/anti-eyebrow, and navel. For each piercing type, it provides:
- Typical anatomy span ranges
- Standard gauge recommendations
- Clearance values for fresh and healed tissue
- Nearest standard internal length sizes

The calculator outputs a specific internal length recommendation (the distance between ball seats, not overall barbell length) along with an optional next-size-up option for extra comfort margin.

## How to Use

### Step 1: Select your piercing type
Choose from the dropdown: Rook, Daith, Snug, Conch (curved), Eyebrow/Anti-eyebrow, or Navel/Belly button. The tool displays typical anatomy span range and standard gauge for your selection.

### Step 2: Choose healing stage
Select "Fresh / unhealed" for new piercings or "Fully healed" for established piercings. Fresh piercings receive larger clearance to accommodate swelling.

### Step 3: Enter anatomy measurement
Input your tissue depth measurement in millimeters (1-40 mm, 0.5 mm increments). Measure the tissue span where the barbell will pass through.

### Step 4: Click calculate
Press the "Calculate Length" button to generate your recommendation.

### Step 5: Review the recommendation
The result shows:
- Recommended internal length in millimeters
- Calculation breakdown (anatomy span + clearance)
- Nearest standard size option
- Next size up option (if available)
- Gauge recommendation
- Healing stage advice

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### What is internal length for a curved barbell?
Internal length is the distance between the two ball seats on the barbell, not the overall length including the balls. This is the measurement that determines fit.

### How do I measure my anatomy span for this calculator?
Measure the tissue depth where the barbell will pass through, from entry point to exit point. Use calipers for accuracy, measuring in millimeters.

### Why do fresh piercings need more clearance?
Fresh piercings swell during healing. The extra clearance prevents the jewelry from embedding or causing pressure on swollen tissue.

### Can I use this calculator for straight barbells?
No. This tool is specifically designed for curved barbells. Straight barbell sizing follows different principles and clearance values.

### What gauge should I use for my curved barbell?
The tool provides gauge recommendations per piercing type. Common gauges are 16g (1.2 mm) for rook, snug, and eyebrow piercings, and 14g (1.6 mm) for daith, conch, and navel piercings.

### When should I downsize my curved barbell?
Downsize only after the piercing is fully healed. A professional piercer should confirm healing before switching to a shorter barbell.

### What materials should I use for initial piercings?
Use implant-grade materials: ASTM F136 titanium or BioFlex polymer for initial piercings. These materials minimize irritation and allergic reactions.

### What if my measurement falls between standard sizes?
The calculator recommends the nearest standard size at or above your minimum requirement. It also shows the next size up for extra comfort margin.

### Is this tool suitable for surface piercings?
The eyebrow/anti-eyebrow option covers surface-type curved barbell piercings through the brow ridge. Other surface piercings may require different sizing approaches.

### Can I trust these measurements for ordering jewelry?
The tool provides a guide based on standard sizing practices. Always verify sizing with a professional piercer before ordering, as individual anatomy varies.
